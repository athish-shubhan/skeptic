# Changelog

## v0.1.0 (2026-10-31)

- Point-in-time store with explicit availability times and as-of alignment.
- Walk-forward, purged K-fold and combinatorial purged cross-validation with path reassembly.
- Sharpe confidence interval (skew and kurtosis adjusted), PSR, deflated Sharpe, minimum track record length.
- Vectorised stationary block bootstrap and group bootstrap.
- Bonferroni, Holm and Benjamini-Hochberg adjustments; probability of backtest overfitting (CSCV).
- SQLite trial ledger with an Optuna callback; HTML tearsheet that refuses to render without a trial count.
- Canary study (noise, planted signal, leakage, best-of-N) and the pre-registered signal zoo on BTCUSDT and ETHUSDT.
